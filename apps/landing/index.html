Questions

Before you make the group.

Or try the free split bill calculator first, no sign-in needed.

Is Splitify free?

Yes. Splitify is free, with no ads and no daily limit on how many expenses you add.

Do my friends need to install an app?

No. Send them the group’s invite link; it opens in any browser and they sign in with Google or a code sent to their email. The Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac apps are optional.

How does Splitify work out who owes whom?

Your balance is what you paid minus your share of every bill. Splitify then finds the fewest payments that settle everyone, and tapping Why? next to any balance shows the sum line by line.

Can we split a bill unequally?

Yes. Split equally, by exact amounts, by percent or by shares, for example by the nights each person stayed. People who weren’t there can be left out of a bill.

Can someone change an amount without the group knowing?

No. Every change keeps its history and shows who changed what. Anyone in the group can suggest a fix with a reason, and the person who added the bill accepts or declines it.

How do we settle up?

Pay each other the way you normally do, by UPI or cash, and record the payment in Splitify. It waits until the person who got the money confirms it. Splitify never moves money itself.

Does it work on iPhone, Windows and Mac?

Yes. On iPhone, open the site in Safari and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Windows and Mac, install it from Chrome or Edge. Android has its own app, a 20 MB download from this page.
