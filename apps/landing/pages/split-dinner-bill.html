How to split a dinner bill fairly

Splitting equally is quick, but not when one person had a salad and another had three drinks. Here’s the fair way, and when equal is good enough.

Updated 27 October 2026 · 4 minute read

When to just split equally

If everyone ordered about the same, or you eat out together often and it evens out, split the total equally. The split bill calculator handles service charge and tip, and gets the shares right to the paisa.

When people ordered differently

Work it out in three steps:

  1. Give each person their own dishes and drinks.
  2. Share the common dishes, like a starter or a dessert for the table, between the people who had them.
  3. Spread tax, service charge and discounts in proportion to what each person ordered. Whoever ordered more pays more of the tax.

A discount works the same way in reverse: 20% off the food takes 20% off each person’s subtotal.

Service charge and tip

If the restaurant adds a service charge, include it in the proportional step above. In India, a service charge on the bill is voluntary, and you can ask for it to be taken off. A tip you leave on top can be split equally or in proportion, whichever the table prefers.

One person paid the whole bill

This is the usual case: one card or one UPI payment at the counter. In the example, if Rahul paid ₹2,200, Sneha owes him ₹440 and Amit owes him ₹1,100. If the same friends eat out often, don’t settle every dinner: record each one and settle every few weeks in the fewest payments.

Questions

Should tax be split equally or by what each person ordered?

By what each person ordered. Tax and service charge are a percent of the food, so whoever ordered more should pay more of them.

How do you split a shared starter?

Divide it equally between the people who ate it, and add that to each of their subtotals before working out tax.

Is service charge compulsory in India?

No. It’s voluntary, and you can ask the restaurant to remove it from the bill. If you pay it, split it in proportion to what each person ordered.
