How the calculator splits a bill

It adds the service charge (as a percent of the bill) and the tip to the bill, then divides the total by the number of people. Amounts are worked out to the paisa, so the shares always add up to exactly the total: when a bill doesn’t divide evenly, one or two people pay one paisa more. Tick Round up to whole rupees to get easier numbers; the calculator shows how much extra that collects.

How “Who paid what” works out the payments

Each person’s share is the total divided by the number of people. Their balance is what they paid minus their share: positive means they get money back, negative means they owe. The calculator then matches the person who owes the most with the person who is owed the most, again and again, until everyone is square. That takes at most one payment fewer than the number of people, and usually fewer.

Questions

Is this split bill calculator free?

Yes. It’s free, works in any browser on your phone or laptop, and needs no sign-in. Nothing you type leaves your device.

How do I split a bill with service charge and tip?

Enter the bill amount, the service charge as a percent and the tip in rupees. The calculator adds them to the bill and divides the total equally between everyone.

What if people ordered different amounts?

Split each person’s own dishes to them, share the common dishes, and spread tax and service charge in proportion to what each person ordered. Our guide to splitting a dinner bill shows how, with an example.

How do I work out who owes whom after a trip?

Use “Who paid what”: enter what each person paid in total. The calculator shows everyone’s share and balance, and the fewest payments that settle the trip.
